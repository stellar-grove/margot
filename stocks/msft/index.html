MSFT is spending 3.2x its depreciation on capital projects, so trailing free cash flow is held down by investment in future capacity. The capex-adjusted value counts only depreciation as the cost of standing still (operating cash flow minus depreciation) and keeps every other input the same: $717.64 per share, with the price implying 11% starting growth on that basis. The truth is likely between the two values.

These inputs are assumptions. A one-point change in the discount rate moves the value a lot, so read the result as a range rather than a target.
